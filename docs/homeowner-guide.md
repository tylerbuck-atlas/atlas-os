# Your Atlas Home — A Short Guide

Welcome. Atlas is the small computer your installer set up that runs your
home's sensors, devices, and — if you enabled it — an AI helper. This is
the one-page version of what you need to know. No technical background
required.

## The big idea

Atlas lives **in your house, on your hardware**. It doesn't use a cloud
account, it doesn't send your information anywhere, and it keeps working
if your internet goes down. Your home's data is yours, stored on your
box.

## What it can do

- Keep track of your devices and sensors — what's on, what's open, how
  warm the hallway is — and remember their history.
- Do things for you: turn lights on, and whatever else you and your
  installer set up.
- Answer questions about your home, if the AI helper is enabled ("is the
  garage door closed?", "how warm is the bedroom?").

## The one rule that keeps it safe

**Atlas never does anything you haven't allowed, and the AI can only
*suggest* — never act on its own.** When the assistant proposes to do
something, it becomes a request that waits for a yes. You (or rules you
set up with your installer) decide what's allowed automatically and what
needs your approval each time. Nothing happens behind your back, and
everything it does is written down in a log.

## Your data is private by design

The most sensitive things — anything from a camera, or whether someone's
home — are locked down by the system itself. The AI helper literally
cannot see them unless you specifically allow it, one permission at a
time. If you have cameras, they take a picture only when something you
asked for needs one, a light shows when they do, and the picture isn't
kept unless you want it.

## If something seems wrong

- **It's a computer with an off switch.** You can unplug it. Nothing bad
  happens to your house — devices just stop being automated until it's
  back on.
- If a device isn't responding or the system seems confused, a restart
  often fixes it — or contact your installer.

## Things worth keeping safe

Your installer gave you a sealed backup of your home's "trust key." Keep
it somewhere safe (a drawer, a safe). If your Atlas box ever dies, that
backup lets a new one take over as *your* system rather than starting
over. You don't need to do anything with it — just don't lose it.

## Your data, your call

You can, at any time, ask your installer (or do it yourself if you're
comfortable) to: show you everything Atlas has stored, delete any of it,
remove a device or camera, or wipe the whole thing and start fresh. It's
your home and your information.
